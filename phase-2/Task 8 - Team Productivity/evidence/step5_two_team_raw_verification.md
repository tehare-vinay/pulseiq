## Method
Raw task-level records were grouped by team and sprint. Completed story points were summed for tasks with status Done. Cycle time used valid Done tasks with start and completion dates and a non-negative date difference. On-time delivery used Done tasks with both due and completion dates, with completion on or before the due date treated as on time.

## Result
For SP-02, the raw-data calculations matched the governed metric views for velocity, cycle time, and on-time delivery for both teams. Cancelled, blocked, and reopened work was checked separately.

## Limitations
This verification demonstrates a match for the selected sprint SP-02; it does not independently prove every metric for every sprint. The dashboard and findings also document source date/cycle-time quality limitations.
